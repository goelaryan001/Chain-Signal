Crypto market & on-chain anomaly detection

ChainSignal Explorer

One year of daily market data for the top 1,000 coins and every on-chain transfer of three tokens, cleaned, turned into features, and run through three anomaly detectors. The goal: find trading that inflates volume without real demand (wash trading), and be honest about what the detectors catch and miss.

Takeaways

  • z-score and Isolation Forest mostly agree on real data. Crypto's fat tails fill the 1% budget with huge outliers: a day needs a robust |z| of at least to make it.
  • LOF sees something different: local and multivariate anomalies, such as a turnover jump with flat volume because market cap fell.

Wash-trading candidates

Wash trading inflates volume without the price movement real buying and selling would cause. Two screens look for that shape. Candidates are leads, not verdicts: only Celer Network was checked against an outside source.

Celer Network, 3 June 2026: $1.5B traded on an $18M coin

That's 82 times the coin's entire market cap in one day, with the price flat, then $487M, then back to about $2M. An independent aggregator (CoinRanking) shows the same: $1.5B, then $513M. So it isn't one data provider's glitch; exchanges really reported that volume. Trading of that size with no price impact can't be genuine demand for an $18M coin. It's consistent with wash trading or fake exchange-reported volume.

Evaluation: planted anomalies and real events

There's no answer key for crypto anomalies, so we used two tests. Synthetic injection: 887 anomalies we created ourselves (one per coin), planted in the raw data at a range of strengths, measured in each coin's own volatility. Backtest: real, independently documented events.

Limitations

  • Survivorship bias: the coin universe is today's top 1,000, which excludes coins that collapsed during the year. The backtest's event coins had to be added separately.
  • Exploits that don't move the token's price are invisible to market-data detectors (Kelp DAO). On-chain protocol outflows would be needed.
  • Small anomalies go undetected: below about 12σ, crypto's own extreme days use up the 1% alert budget.
  • Smart-contract wallets (Safe, ERC-4337) are labelled as contracts, so wash trading routed through them would be missed.
  • The on-chain sample is small: 3 tokens over 90 days.
  • Candidates are not verdicts. Only Celer Network was confirmed with a second source.
